## Executive Summary & Evaluation Framework

The venture capital ecosystem in East Africa, centered in Nairobi, Kenya, has matured into the premier technology capital allocation hub on the African continent [1]. In recent venture cycles, Kenya led continental capital formation with over $1.04 billion in total funding raised [1]. While historical funding in East Africa concentrated on consumer fintech and off-grid climate solutions, a structural shift is underway toward enterprise B2B software, Software-as-a-Service (SaaS), PropTech, cybersecurity, and physical-digital infrastructure [1, 2, 3].

**GatePass** operates at the intersection of B2B SaaS, PropTech, and access-control security technology [2, 3]. As an early-commercial stage startup possessing a functional, repository-verified product (535 frontend tests, 578 server tests, and verified offline-first guard console architecture) but limited real-world deployment data [380, 385, 393], GatePass occupies a specific risk-return profile. 

To evaluate potential investor fit for GatePass without relying on prestige bias or unverified databases, candidate funds are assessed across five objective structural dimensions [115]:
1. **Stage Alignment:** Preference for active pre-seed and seed-stage investors willing to deploy initial capital checks ($25,000 to $300,000) into early-commercial startups [6, 9, 10, 96].
2. **Sector Thesis Synergy:** Direct thesis focus on B2B SaaS, enterprise productivity software, PropTech, cybersecurity, access control, or foundational digital infrastructure [2, 3, 8, 11, 96].
3. **Deployment Risk Tolerance:** Willingness to evaluate startups based on technical product architecture, addressable market size, and team capability rather than requiring mature historical Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) [6, 7, 8, 96].
4. **Geographic Proximity & Regional Footprint:** Established investment presence or active portfolio deployments in Kenya and East Africa, ensuring contextual understanding of local commercial real estate and private security sales cycles [1, 12, 13, 14, 96].
5. **Venture Building & Strategic Network Value:** Availability of dedicated platform support, corporate networks, or structured accelerator residencies capable of helping GatePass secure its initial 3 to 5 commercial property deployments [6, 7, 8, 96].
